Easy Housing Solutions (EHS) — Peter Chen Standard Model Notation (Rendered from Markdown)
Rectangle = Strong Entity Double Rectangle = Weak Entity Diamond = Relationship Ellipse = Attribute Gold Underlined = Primary Key 1 / N / M = Cardinality Ratio
